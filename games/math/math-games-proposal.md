# Math Section Game Proposal

## Section identity

- **English:** Math
- **Arabic:** رياضيات
- **Target age:** 9–11 (Grade 5 primary, adaptable 7–12)
- **Subject:** Mathematics — aligned with Grade 5 Semester 1 (Cambridge / Oman MOE)

## Proposed games

| # | ID | English name | Arabic name | Math focus | Core mechanic | Cultural theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `number-vault` | Number Vault | خزنة الأرقام | Place value, comparing, rounding, sequences | Build the correct 6-digit code to open vault doors | Omani fort treasure vault |
| 2 | `souq-arithmetics` | Souq Arithmetic | حسابات السوق | Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, order of operations | Run a market stall: calculate customer totals, give change, restock | Traditional GCC souq |
| 3 | `falcon-grid` | Falcon Grid | شبكة الصقر | Coordinates, geometry, translations, reflections | Move a falcon across a map by reading and plotting coordinates | Desert falconry / Oman map |
| 4 | `fort-builder` | Fort Builder | باني القلعة | 2D/3D shapes, nets, area, perimeter | Construct traditional forts and houses from shape nets; measure walls | Omani forts and architecture |
| 5 | `time-caravan` | Time Caravan | قافلة الزمن | Time (12/24 h), duration, timetables | Plan a caravan journey across GCC cities; catch buses and boats on time | GCC travel / Oman transport |
| 6 | `falcon-coins` | Falcon Coins | عملات الصقر | Decimals, money, fractions of a riyal | Buy and sell falconry goods; handle riyals and baisa accurately | Falcon market / traditional trade |

## Notes

- Each game maps directly to one or more lessons in the Grade 5 book.
- Games are designed as **short-session educational mini-games** (2–4 minutes), single-player first, with local/practice modes.
- Art style: bright, flat-shaded, low-poly 3D consistent with the existing Sahara Play visual direction.
- All visuals procedural; no external model/texture/audio assets required.
